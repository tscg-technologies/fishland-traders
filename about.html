HYGIENIC COLD CHAIN

How we maintain ocean freshness

From harbor landing to your kitchen, our systematic handling ensures zero temperature breaks.

1. Selective Grading

Only firm, clear-eyed catches meeting premium standards are accepted at harbor sorting stations.

2. Chilled Packing

Layered with food-grade crushed ice in insulated, sealed containers to prevent spoilage and retain moisture.

3. Express Transit

Overnight temperature-controlled transit ensures seafood reaches Hyderabad before daybreak for immediate distribution.

4. Hygienic Dispatch

Clean, sanitized workstations in Uppal handle custom cutting, descaling, gutting, and immediate wholesale dispatch.
