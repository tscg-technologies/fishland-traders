WHOLESALE & RETAIL SUPPLY

Delivering Oceanic Freshness to Hyderabad Every Single Day

Fish Land Traders is committed to delivering daily fresh seafood and premium frozen products through reliable wholesale supply. We focus on hygienic processing, premium quality, and customer satisfaction in every order.

Established as a dedicated seafood supply hub in Hyderabad, Fish Land Traders bridges prime coastal landing centers with the city's bustling food service sector. We procure catches directly from authorized landing centers in Visakhapatnam, Chennai, and Kochi, alongside reputable freshwater aquaculture farms in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

Whether it is prime Seer Fish (Vanjaram), sweet Indian carps (Rohu & Catla), the official Telangana State Fish Murrel (Korrameenu), export-grade Tiger Prawns, or lagoon Mud Crabs, our cold-chain network ensures that what arrives at your kitchen door retains pristine texture, aroma, and natural taste.
